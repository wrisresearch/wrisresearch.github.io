People & Automation

“As we build ever more sophisticated organizational capabilities, the speed of decision-making increases … the human interface snaps into sharper focus” (Deloitte, “Resilient Operations”). While automation excels at routine tasks, human judgment remains vital in responding to unforeseen events. Freight forwarders negotiate with carriers, adjust shipping routes, and manage exceptions, while logisticians coordinate across warehouses and transportation networks.

The integration of AI and machine learning enhances human capability rather than replacing it. Employees interpret alerts, decide on alternative actions, and ensure compliance, all while overseeing automated recommendations. Operational resilience depends on this synergy, where human expertise, digital orchestration, and real-time data intersect to maintain efficiency and agility in global trade (Blue Yonder and Ernst & Young LLP 6).
